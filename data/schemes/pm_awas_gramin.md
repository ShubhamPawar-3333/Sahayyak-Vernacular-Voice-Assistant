# PM Awas Yojana - Gramin (पीएम आवास योजना - ग्रामीण)

> **Sources:** [pmayg.nic.in](https://pmayg.nic.in) | [Ministry of Rural Development](https://rural.gov.in)
> **Last Verified:** February 2026 | **Data Accuracy:** Official government portal

## Overview
- **Full Name:** Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Gramin (PMAY-G)
- **Type:** Central Government - Housing
- **Department:** Ministry of Rural Development
- **Launch Year:** 2016
- **Status:** Active

## Benefits
- Financial assistance for building a pucca house
- Plain areas: ₹1,20,000
- Hilly/difficult areas: ₹1,30,000
- Additional ₹12,000 for toilet construction (from Swachh Bharat Mission)
- 90/95 days of unskilled labour under MGNREGA

## Eligibility
- Houseless families or living in kutcha/dilapidated houses
- Family identified in SECC 2011 data
- Priority: SC/ST, freed bonded labour, minorities, single women

### Not Eligible
- Families owning a pucca house
- Any family member is a government employee
- Any family member pays income tax
- Families owning motorized vehicles/fishing boats
- Families owning KVP/3+ acres irrigated land

## Required Documents
1. Aadhaar card (आधार कार्ड)
2. BPL certificate
3. Job card (MGNREGA)
4. Bank account details
5. Land documents or NOC from Gram Panchayat
6. Passport-size photo

## How to Apply
1. Approach Gram Panchayat office
2. Apply through Gram Sabha
3. Gram Panchayat verifies and recommends
4. Block Development Officer approves
5. Application sent to district level
6. Beneficiary list published
7. Construction begins with geo-tagged progress tracking

## मराठी माहिती
पीएम आवास योजना ग्रामीण अंतर्गत पक्के घर बांधण्यासाठी ₹1,20,000 मदत दिली जाते.
डोंगराळ भागात ₹1,30,000 मदत मिळते.
शौचालय बांधणीसाठी अतिरिक्त ₹12,000 मिळतात.
