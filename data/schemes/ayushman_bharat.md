# Ayushman Bharat - PM Jan Arogya Yojana (आयुष्मान भारत - पीएम जन आरोग्य योजना)

> **Sources:** [pmjay.gov.in](https://pmjay.gov.in) | [NHA Official](https://nha.gov.in)
> **Last Verified:** February 2026 | **Data Accuracy:** Official government portal

## Overview
- **Full Name:** Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY)
- **Type:** Central Government - Health Insurance
- **Department:** Ministry of Health & Family Welfare
- **Launch Year:** 2018
- **Status:** Active

## Benefits
- Health insurance cover of ₹5,00,000 per family per year
- Covers hospitalization expenses
- Free treatment at empaneled hospitals (public & private)
- 1,929+ medical procedures covered
- No age bar or family size limit
- Pre and post hospitalization expenses covered (3 days pre, 15 days post)

## Eligibility
- Families identified in SECC 2011 database
- Below Poverty Line (BPL) families
- Rural: Based on 7 deprivation criteria
- Urban: Based on 11 occupational categories

### Auto-Eligible Categories
- SC/ST households
- Families with no adult member aged 16-59
- Families with no male earning member
- Manual scavenger families
- Primitive tribal groups
- Bonded labour families

## Required Documents
1. Aadhaar card (आधार कार्ड)
2. Ration card (रेशन कार्ड)
3. SECC database entry proof
4. Income certificate
5. Category certificate (SC/ST/OBC if applicable)
6. Passport-size photo

## How to Apply / Check Eligibility
1. Visit: https://pmjay.gov.in
2. Enter mobile number to check if your family is listed
3. If eligible, visit nearest Ayushman Mitra at empaneled hospital
4. Show Aadhaar card for e-KYC verification
5. Receive Ayushman Card (e-card)

### In Maharashtra
- Visit Aapla Sarkar Seva Kendra
- Contact Ayushman Mitra at any government hospital
- Call Maharashtra health helpline

## मराठी माहिती
आयुष्मान भारत योजनेत प्रत्येक कुटुंबाला दरवर्षी ₹5 लाखांपर्यंत मोफत उपचार मिळतात.
या योजनेत 1,929 पेक्षा जास्त वैद्यकीय प्रक्रिया समाविष्ट आहेत.
कुटुंबातील सदस्य संख्येवर कोणतीही मर्यादा नाही.
