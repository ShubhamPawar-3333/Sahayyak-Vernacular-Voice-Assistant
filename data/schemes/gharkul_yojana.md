# Gharkul Yojana - Maharashtra (घरकुल योजना)

> **Sources:** [sjsa.maharashtra.gov.in](https://sjsa.maharashtra.gov.in) | [Maharashtra Social Justice Dept](https://sjsa.maharashtra.gov.in)
> **Last Verified:** February 2026 | **Data Accuracy:** Official state government portal

## Overview
- **Full Name:** Ramai Awas Gharkul Yojana
- **Type:** Maharashtra State Government - Housing for SC/NT/SBC
- **Department:** Social Justice & Special Assistance Department, Maharashtra
- **Launch Year:** Ongoing (periodic revisions)
- **Status:** Active

## Benefits
- Financial assistance for constructing a house
- ₹2,50,000 for house construction
- Additional ₹12,000 for toilet (under SBM)
- House built on own land or allotted government land

## Eligibility
- Scheduled Caste (SC) communities
- Nomadic Tribes (NT)
- Special Backward Classes (SBC)
- De-notified Tribes (DNT)
- Must be a Maharashtra resident
- Houseless or living in kutcha house
- Name should be in Gram Panchayat Awaas list

### Income Criteria
- Annual income below ₹1,00,000 (rural)
- Annual income below ₹1,50,000 (urban)

## Required Documents
1. Aadhaar card (आधार कार्ड)
2. Caste certificate (जात प्रमाणपत्र)
3. Income certificate (उत्पन्नाचा दाखला)
4. Domicile certificate (अधिवास प्रमाणपत्र)
5. Land ownership proof or allotment letter
6. BPL card / ration card
7. Bank account passbook
8. Passport-size photo

## How to Apply
1. Apply at Gram Panchayat (rural) or Municipal office (urban)
2. Submit application with required documents
3. Verification by Talathi and Gram Sevak
4. Recommended by Gram Panchayat resolution
5. Approved at District Social Welfare office
6. Fund released in installments based on construction progress

## मराठी माहिती
रमाई आवास घरकुल योजना ही SC/NT/SBC समुदायातील कुटुंबांसाठी आहे.
घर बांधणीसाठी ₹2,50,000 आर्थिक मदत दिली जाते.
ग्रामपंचायत मार्फत अर्ज करावा.
बांधकामाच्या प्रगतीनुसार निधी टप्प्याटप्प्याने दिला जातो.
