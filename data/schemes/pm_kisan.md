# PM Kisan Samman Nidhi (पीएम किसान सन्मान निधी)

> **Sources:** [pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in) | [PIB Press Release](https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1564645)
> **Last Verified:** February 2026 | **Data Accuracy:** Official government portal

## Overview
- **Full Name:** Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi
- **Type:** Central Government - Direct Benefit Transfer
- **Department:** Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare
- **Launch Year:** 2019
- **Status:** Active

## Benefits
- ₹6,000 per year in 3 installments of ₹2,000 each
- Directly transferred to bank account via DBT
- Installment 1: April-July
- Installment 2: August-November
- Installment 3: December-March

## Eligibility
- All landholding farmer families in India
- Must have cultivable land in their name
- Aadhaar card mandatory
- Bank account linked to Aadhaar

### Not Eligible
- Income tax payers
- Government employees (current or retired)
- Professionals like doctors, lawyers, engineers earning above ₹10,000/month
- Institutional landholders

## Required Documents
1. Aadhaar card (आधार कार्ड)
2. Bank passbook (बँक पासबुक)
3. Land ownership documents (7/12 उतारा / सातबारा)
4. Caste certificate (if applicable)
5. Mobile number linked to Aadhaar

## How to Apply
1. Visit official portal: https://pmkisan.gov.in
2. Click "New Farmer Registration"
3. Enter Aadhaar number
4. Fill farmer details and land information
5. Upload required documents
6. Submit application

### Offline Application
- Visit nearest Common Service Center (CSC)
- Contact local Patwari or Revenue Officer
- Apply through Agriculture Department office
