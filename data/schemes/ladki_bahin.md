# Ladki Bahin Yojana - Maharashtra (लाडकी बहीण योजना)

> **Sources:** [ladkibahin.maharashtra.gov.in](https://ladkibahin.maharashtra.gov.in) | [Maharashtra Women & Child Development](https://womenchild.maharashtra.gov.in)
> **Last Verified:** February 2026 | **Data Accuracy:** Official state government portal

## Overview
- **Full Name:** Mukhyamantri Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana
- **Type:** Maharashtra State Government - Women Welfare
- **Department:** Department of Women & Child Development, Maharashtra
- **Launch Year:** 2024
- **Status:** Active

## Benefits
- ₹1,500 per month (₹18,000 per year) per eligible woman
- Directly transferred to bank account via DBT
- Monthly transfer on designated dates

## Eligibility
- Women aged 21 to 65 years
- Maharashtra domicile
- Annual family income below ₹2,50,000
- Must not be a government employee
- Must not be an income tax payer

### Eligible Categories
- Married women
- Divorced women (विभक्त)
- Widows (विधवा)
- Abandoned women (परित्यक्ता)
- Destitute women (निराधार)

## Required Documents
1. Aadhaar card (आधार कार्ड)
2. Domicile certificate (अधिवास प्रमाणपत्र)
3. Income certificate below ₹2,50,000 (उत्पन्नाचा दाखला)
4. Bank account passbook (स्वतःच्या नावाचे)
5. Ration card (रेशन कार्ड)
6. Passport-size photo
7. Age proof (birth certificate or school leaving)

## How to Apply
1. Visit Nari Shakti Doot app or portal
2. Fill online application with Aadhaar
3. Upload required documents
4. Application verified at Gram Panchayat/Ward level
5. Approved by Taluka/District office
6. Amount credited monthly

### Offline Application
- Apply at Anganwadi center
- Apply at Gram Panchayat office
- Apply at District Women & Child Development office

## मराठी माहिती
मुख्यमंत्री माझी लाडकी बहीण योजनेत पात्र महिलांना दरमहा ₹1,500 मिळतात.
21 ते 65 वयोगटातील महिला पात्र आहेत.
कुटुंबाचे वार्षिक उत्पन्न ₹2,50,000 पेक्षा कमी असावे.
विवाहित, विधवा, परित्यक्ता, निराधार महिला या सर्व पात्र आहेत.
