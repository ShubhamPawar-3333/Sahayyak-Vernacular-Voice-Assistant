# Shetkari Samman Yojana - Maharashtra (शेतकरी सन्मान योजना)

> **Sources:** [krishi.maharashtra.gov.in](https://krishi.maharashtra.gov.in) | [pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in)
> **Last Verified:** February 2026 | **Data Accuracy:** Official state government portal

## Overview
- **Full Name:** Namo Shetkari Mahasanman Nidhi Yojana
- **Type:** Maharashtra State Government - Farmer Support
- **Department:** Department of Agriculture, Maharashtra
- **Launch Year:** 2023
- **Status:** Active

## Benefits
- ₹6,000 per year (additional to PM Kisan)
- Combined with PM Kisan: ₹12,000 per year total
- PM Kisan: ₹6,000 (Central) + Namo Shetkari: ₹6,000 (State)
- Transferred in 3 installments of ₹2,000 each
- Directly to Aadhaar-linked bank account

## Eligibility
- Must be a registered farmer in Maharashtra
- Must be a PM Kisan beneficiary
- Land must be in farmer's name (7/12 extract)
- Must have valid Aadhaar linked to bank account

### Not Eligible
- Income tax payers
- Government employees
- Land not in applicant's name
- Those not registered under PM Kisan

## Required Documents
1. Aadhaar card (आधार कार्ड)
2. Bank account linked to Aadhaar
3. 7/12 extract (सातबारा उतारा)
4. 8-A extract
5. PM Kisan registration proof

## How to Apply
- **No separate application needed**
- Automatically eligible if registered under PM Kisan
- Amount credited along with PM Kisan installments
- Check status on PM Kisan portal

## मराठी माहिती
नमो शेतकरी महासन्मान निधी योजनेंतर्गत शेतकऱ्यांना दरवर्षी ₹6,000 अतिरिक्त मिळतात.
पीएम किसान योजनेसोबत एकूण ₹12,000 प्रतिवर्ष मिळतात.
वेगळा अर्ज करण्याची गरज नाही - पीएम किसान लाभार्थ्यांना स्वयंचलितपणे लाभ मिळतो.
