# Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (मनरेगा)

> **Sources:** [nrega.nic.in](https://nrega.nic.in) | [Ministry of Rural Development](https://rural.gov.in)
> **Last Verified:** February 2026 | **Data Accuracy:** Official government portal

## Overview
- **Full Name:** Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA)
- **Type:** Central Government - Employment Guarantee
- **Department:** Ministry of Rural Development
- **Launch Year:** 2005
- **Status:** Active

## Benefits
- 100 days of guaranteed wage employment per year
- Per household, not per person
- Daily wage as per state notification
- Maharashtra daily wage: ₹273 (2024-25)
- Unemployment allowance if work not provided within 15 days
- Work within 5 km radius of village

## Eligibility
- Any adult member of a rural household
- Must be willing to do unskilled manual work
- Must apply for work at Gram Panchayat
- Must have a Job Card

### Who Can Apply
- All rural households (no income limit)
- Both men and women eligible
- At least 1/3 of beneficiaries must be women

## Required Documents
1. Job Card (जॉब कार्ड)
2. Aadhaar card (आधार कार्ड)
3. Bank account or Post Office account
4. Application demanding work (written)
5. Passport-size photo

## How to Get Job Card
1. Apply to Gram Panchayat for Job Card
2. Submit application with photo and Aadhaar
3. Gram Panchayat issues Job Card within 15 days
4. Job Card is free of cost

## How to Demand Work
1. Submit written application to Gram Panchayat
2. Gram Panchayat acknowledges with dated receipt
3. Work must be provided within 15 days
4. If no work provided, unemployment allowance is paid

## Types of Work
- Water conservation (तलाव, बांध)
- Drought proofing
- Land development
- Flood control
- Rural connectivity (roads)
- Renovation of water bodies
- Individual land development for SC/ST/BPL

## मराठी माहिती
मनरेगा अंतर्गत ग्रामीण भागातील प्रत्येक कुटुंबाला वर्षातून 100 दिवस रोजगाराची हमी मिळते.
महाराष्ट्रात दररोजची मजुरी ₹273 आहे.
काम 5 किलोमीटर अंतरात दिले जाते. काम न मिळाल्यास बेरोजगारी भत्ता मिळतो.
