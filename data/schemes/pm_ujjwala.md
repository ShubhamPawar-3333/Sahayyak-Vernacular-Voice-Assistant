# PM Ujjwala Yojana (पीएम उज्ज्वला योजना)

> **Sources:** [pmuy.gov.in](https://www.pmuy.gov.in) | [Ministry of Petroleum](https://mopng.gov.in)
> **Last Verified:** February 2026 | **Data Accuracy:** Official government portal

## Overview
- **Full Name:** Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY)
- **Type:** Central Government - LPG Connection
- **Department:** Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas
- **Launch Year:** 2016
- **Status:** Active (Ujjwala 2.0 from 2021)

## Benefits
- Free LPG gas connection
- ₹1,600 subsidy for first refill and stove
- Security deposit for gas cylinder waived
- Free regulator and gas hose
- Ujjwala 2.0: No address proof needed (self-declaration accepted)

## Eligibility
- Women belonging to BPL households
- Not having an LPG connection in the household already
- Age: 18 years and above
- Ujjwala 2.0: Extended to migrant families

### Priority Categories
- SC/ST households
- Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) beneficiaries
- Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) beneficiaries
- Forest dwellers
- Inhabitants of islands and river islands
- Tea & ex-tea garden tribes
- SECC beneficiaries

## Required Documents
1. Aadhaar card of applicant (woman)
2. BPL ration card or certificate
3. Bank account passbook
4. Passport-size photo
5. Proof of address (or self-declaration in Ujjwala 2.0)

## How to Apply
1. Visit nearest LPG distributor (HP, BPCL, or IOCL)
2. Fill Ujjwala application form (KYC form available at distributor)
3. Submit Aadhaar and BPL card
4. LPG connection issued after verification
5. First cylinder refill subsidized

### Online Application
- Website: https://www.pmuy.gov.in
- Apply through CSC (Common Service Center)

## मराठी माहिती
पीएम उज्ज्वला योजनेत गरीब कुटुंबातील महिलांना मोफत एलपीजी गॅस कनेक्शन दिले जाते.
₹1,600 अनुदान पहिल्या सिलिंडर रिफिलसाठी दिले जाते.
उज्ज्वला 2.0 मध्ये पत्त्याचा पुरावा म्हणून स्वघोषणापत्र पुरेसे आहे.
