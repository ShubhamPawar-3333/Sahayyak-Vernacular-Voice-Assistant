# Mahatma Jyotirao Phule Jan Arogya Yojana (महात्मा ज्योतिराव फुले जन आरोग्य योजना)

> **Sources:** [jeevandayee.gov.in](https://www.jeevandayee.gov.in) | [Maharashtra Public Health Dept](https://arogya.maharashtra.gov.in)
> **Last Verified:** February 2026 | **Data Accuracy:** Official state government portal

## Overview
- **Full Name:** Mahatma Jyotirao Phule Jan Arogya Yojana (MJPJAY)
- **Type:** Maharashtra State Government - Health Insurance
- **Department:** Department of Public Health, Maharashtra
- **Launch Year:** 2012 (upgraded regularly)
- **Status:** Active (integrated with Ayushman Bharat)

## Benefits
- Health insurance coverage of ₹1,50,000 per family per year
- Combined with Ayushman Bharat: up to ₹5,00,000
- 1,000+ medical and surgical procedures covered
- Cashless treatment at empaneled hospitals
- Covers pre-existing diseases
- Transport allowance: ₹200 per visit

## Eligibility
- Maharashtra domicile
- Yellow / Orange / White ration card holders
- Annual income below ₹1,00,000
- Farmers listed in 7/12 extract or 8-A
- Recognized journalists

### Priority
- BPL families
- Small and marginal farmers
- Unorganized workers

## Required Documents
1. Aadhaar card (आधार कार्ड)
2. Ration card (yellow/orange/white)
3. Income certificate (उत्पन्नाचा दाखला)
4. Domicile certificate (अधिवास प्रमाणपत्र)
5. Farmer: 7/12 extract (सातबारा उतारा)

## How to Avail
1. Visit empaneled hospital with Aadhaar and ration card
2. Meet Arogya Mitra at hospital
3. E-KYC verification done at hospital
4. Cashless treatment authorized
5. No upfront payment needed

## Covered Procedures (Examples)
- Heart surgery (bypass, angioplasty)
- Kidney transplant
- Cancer treatment (chemotherapy, radiation)
- Joint replacement (knee, hip)
- Brain surgery
- Burns treatment
- Dialysis

## मराठी माहिती
या योजनेत महाराष्ट्रातील कुटुंबांना ₹1.5 लाखांपर्यंत मोफत वैद्यकीय उपचार मिळतात.
आयुष्मान भारत सोबत एकूण ₹5 लाखांपर्यंत कव्हरेज मिळते.
पिवळे, केशरी आणि पांढरे शिधापत्रिका धारक पात्र आहेत.
